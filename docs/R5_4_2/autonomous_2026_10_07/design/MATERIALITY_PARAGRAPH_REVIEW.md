# Materiality assessment model mapping

Authority: complete Data section 30, its Registry assessment row, OD018 scope representation and OD035 finite ASSESSOR dispatch. This review maps the proposed model; it does not establish trusted execution, acceptance or qualification.

Paragraphs 0–4 define the collector context. CollectionRun accounting and capability support remain independent of materiality. Neither successful collection nor unsupported language/framework establishes NON_MATERIAL. Collector completeness cannot be improved by an assessment label or unauthorized exclusion. The complete collector mapping is retained in COLLECTOR_TOOL_PARAGRAPH_REVIEW.md.

Paragraphs 5–6 require a separate canonical assessment with exact subject, assessment input cut, materiality policy, scope, supporting facts, result, rationale and reason codes. The proposed subject domain is surface_key, surface_record, invariant_revision or scope_state_record. Content-or-prior subjects and supporting facts require exact resolution and the complete same-commit DAG rules; the accepted assessment cut and governing policy are independently resolved. OD018 scope references are prior accepted only. An arbitrary caller supplied policy or assessment principal cannot authorize itself.

OD035 extends the existing finite ASSESSOR context with the exact successor materiality_assessment key and the MATERIAL/NON_MATERIAL/UNKNOWN/CONFLICTED output domain. It adds no parallel purpose or runtime authority. The selected assessment kind must select its own output domain; the union of all assessor outputs is insufficient for this predicate. Current applicability, authorized principal and opposing evidence accounting remain mandatory, together with policy applicability to the exact subject and scope. Profile grammar controls prove only that a declared context can be represented.

Paragraph 7 requires UNKNOWN and CONFLICTED to remain unresolved in every denominator and eligibility consumer. A change requires a new immutable assessment instance under the applicable revision contract; consumers bind an exact accepted assessment and cannot select latest by logical ID. Historical inventory or coverage denominators retain their original accepted basis. Conflict resolution and scope exclusion have their own decision authority and cannot be inferred from NON_MATERIAL. Paragraph 8 is a separator.

Remaining proof: independent installed policy/context selection; actual trusted assessor execution; exact accepted-cut visibility and policy/subject/scope equality; same-commit ordering and substitution rejection; assessment revision applicability and immutable historical denominator consumers; missing/conflicted facts and every failure precedence; durable rejection, retries and concurrency; migration and fresh candidate qualification. Full semantic parity remains pending until these obligations have concrete, bound evidence.
