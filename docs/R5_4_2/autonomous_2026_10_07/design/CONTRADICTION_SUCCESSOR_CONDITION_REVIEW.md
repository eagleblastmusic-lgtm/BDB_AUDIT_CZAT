# Resolution belongs to a successor contradiction revision

Complete authoritative Data section 60 states that resolution_decision_ref may occur only in a successor contradiction revision and points to an already accepted resolution decision. Its explicit DAG is prior revision N, decision referring to N, then successor N+1 with predecessor N and that decision. Reopen produces a new successor with predecessor and new evidence, preserving the old decision.

The unqualified contradiction_revision/2 body grammar allowed resolution_decision_ref in an initial revision without a predecessor. It now conditionally requires predecessor_contradiction_revision_ref and contradiction_revision at least 2 when resolution_decision_ref is present. This is the smallest body-shape consequence of the exact source rule. A successor without a new decision remains representable, including reopen. Historical /1 schema and normative source bytes are unchanged.

Six controls cover initial absence/presence, missing predecessor, valid successor shape, first revision despite predecessor, and successor without new resolution. The broader draft conditional suite also adds twelve initialization/noninitialization branch controls against the successor's finite command enum; all 90 retained snapshots replay successfully. RECORD_FOUNDATION_FACT is a legacy test command, not an allowed successor command token; the new grammar control correctly uses ACCEPT_ARTIFACTS.

Schema shape alone cannot prove successor history. Runtime must resolve exact prior predecessor and decision at the declared input cut, require equal contradiction identity, correct N+1 progression and decision's exact prior_revision_ref, applicable resolved scope and resulting status, and no future backlink. Reopen must retain its new qualified counterevidence. Majority or support count is never resolution authority. Trusted production, full DAG, durable rejection and fresh qualification remain open. No gate is promoted by these syntax controls.
