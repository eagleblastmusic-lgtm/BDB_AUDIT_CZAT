# Inventory, disposition and scope-state model mapping

Complete Data section 28 governs inventory_revision, input_disposition_record and scope_state_record. The existing SCOPE_EXCLUSION_SEMANTIC_REVIEW.md supplies the independent approval and exclusion requirements. These mappings do not establish accepted inventory, collector completeness or qualification.

Paragraphs 0–3 require an immutable inventory revision retaining its exact source generation, collector profiles, assigned inputs, disposition and scope records, surfaces, manual/runtime additions, unresolved scope and basis cut. Every dependency must resolve under its declared reference class and exact source context. The accepted inventory selects the supporting facts; neither fact type is a competing current inventory authority.

Paragraphs 4–7 define the six-value InputDisposition domain. PROVISIONAL is permitted only in intermediate inventory and cannot satisfy terminal AF-INV-1 accounting, StageCompletion or STOP. COLLECTED, UNSUPPORTED, EXCLUDED, COLLECTION_FAILED and PARSING_FAILED retain their actual input outcomes. Terminal accounting is distinct from coverage success. EXCLUDED requires independent applicable authorization; unsupported capability cannot provide it.

Paragraphs 8–10 define the separate eight-value ScopeState domain. PROVISIONAL_SCOPE must never enter disposition fields or satisfy terminal scope gates. UNKNOWN_SCOPE retains a material area without complete mapping. EXCLUDED_SCOPE requires a prior accepted scope decision; failed, unsupported, unknown and N/A states provide no coverage PASS. A materiality UNKNOWN/CONFLICTED result cannot remove an unresolved scope from accounting.

Paragraphs 11–14 define the immutable supporting facts. Each disposition points to an exact assigned input. The referencing inventory independently establishes membership and exactly one applicable current disposition per assigned input; a set of unique object references alone is insufficient. A stable scope_key identifies the denominator entry and any scope_ref must agree with it. Exact prior cuts and approval visibility remain mandatory. The excluded branch requires the decision; other states cannot carry a decision to bypass evidence. Scope-key subjects and source-member manifests are prior accepted under OD018, while explicitly permitted content-or-prior basis facts still require a valid union DAG.

Paragraph 15 requires canonical sorting and duplicate rejection of exact disposition and scope-state reference sets. Ordering is by full typed-reference identity, including exact schema identity and digest profile. Sorting object IDs alone or merging records with different full references is insufficient. Membership and one-disposition-per-input remain separate cross-object predicates.

Paragraph 16 requires a new inventory revision for a late discovered material subsystem. Current stage/STOP applicability must account for the newer accepted source/scope basis and can invalidate conclusions based on the old cut. Immutable historical inventories and conclusions remain retained with their exact original basis; a mutable latest-by-ID fallback is forbidden. Paragraph 17 is a separator.

Outstanding proof: independently qualified collector/accounting context and invocation; actual source/input membership and complete enumeration; no duplicate input outcomes, silent omission or unsupported-to-excluded laundering; exact scope-key/ref/source equality; authorized exclusion purpose/action/scope; accepted cuts and complete same-commit DAG; canonical sorting with full identity; inventory revision selection and downstream invalidation; retry/conflict and durable failure precedence; migration and candidate qualification. Existing finite syntax/conditional controls do not close these semantic obligations.
