# Knowledge, corpus and projection model mapping

Complete Data sections 22, 23, 24, 26, 27 and 105, together with the complete 14.3 initial-grant/KnowledgeState dependency section, govern this mapping. These are static design conclusions, not actual capture, isolation or accepted-history evidence.

Data 22 paragraphs 0–4 separate the derived exposure ledger from accepted grants, exposure, delivery observations and assessments. Event-enriched fields are projections: delivery profile is dereferenced from the accepted grant context and effective order comes from the external accepting commit/ordinal. They are not additional exposure body fields. Paragraphs 5–6 prohibit removing exposure for missing ACK and prohibit claims about psychological knowledge. The grant/exposure/knowledge DAG uses actual content edges and prior input cuts; no body predicts its own accepting head.

The complete 14.3 dependency contract permits initial Attempt → Grant → Exposure → KnowledgeState content in one topologically ordered commit. OD025 requires Grant's explicit null initial predecessor; Exposure's predecessor remains optional, consistently with its separate declared body. A later predecessor must be the exact prior accepted KnowledgeState. Exposure and grant refer to the same exact attempt/view/channel/context. Initial knowledge has no future Assignment backlink; assignment follows knowledge. Same-commit content permission never supplies prior acceptance provenance or independent actor authorization.

Data 22 paragraphs 7–10 require contamination to retain exact attempt/cut, forbidden-knowledge policy, channel inventory, exposures, matches, channel observations, scope, limitations and reason codes. CLEAN applies only to completely assessed, qualified channels and scope. Missing inventory or unverifiable capture produces UNKNOWN, not temporary CLEAN or NOT_APPLICABLE. Paragraphs 11–14 separately require isolation qualification with exact executor/delivery context and positive evidence for every required filesystem/network/tool/session channel. ENFORCED, DECLARED and UNKNOWN are isolation levels; CONTAMINATED remains a distinct assessment result. Required empty or unverifiable channel evidence cannot produce ENFORCED. Paragraph 15 is a separator.

Data 23 paragraphs 0–2 establish KnowledgeState as an immutable, attempt-bound canonical revision with exact prior predecessor when present, input cut, allowed views, exposures, contamination and isolation qualification. Discovery never points to an ephemeral recalculated UI view. Paragraphs 3–6 retain the separate three-value isolation assurance domain and actual boundary-evidence requirement. Missing proof stays DECLARED/UNKNOWN. Paragraph 7 is a separator.

Data 24 paragraphs 0–3 require exact immutable corpus members and admission assessments on the represented cut, role, consumer stage and source. Changed membership or current admissibility creates a new revision; a corpus is not an alternative lineage authority. Paragraph 4 is a separator.

Data 26 paragraphs 0–5 separate member candidate from later consumer/role-specific admission. Admission selects the prior exact candidate, consumer stage, knowledge policy, input cut, exposure/knowledge and additional evidence, finite result, limitations and reasons. The candidate has no admission backlink. The manifest selects both exact candidates and corresponding assessments. Membership cannot automatically supply truth, independence, depth or blind origin. Paragraph 6 is a separator. The companion approved corpus_member_candidate proposal supplies its explicit body and tagged raw/object representation and must retain its own independent source mapping.

Data 27 paragraphs 0–5 define positive-view projection: only explicitly permitted fields and metadata survive, including controls on names, hashes, producer identity, severity, support count, prior corpus labels and resolvable evidence. A raw locator cannot bypass capability brokerage. The entire resolver closure is checked; a reachable forbidden artifact rejects the view. Possible prior delivery additionally requires retained exposure and contamination. The view bytes have no own raw digest. The successor ViewManifest/2 representation is the OD017 projection over exact RawRefs and finite metadata, with the explicit retained payload/parser contract; it does not grant arbitrary canonical claims or wider resolver capability. Paragraph 6 is a separator.

Data 105 paragraphs 0–2 complete the native snapshot requirements: disposable/rebuildable projection or immutable historical artifact; exact as-of head, projector code/profile and retained input references; stale snapshots cannot service a gate. Deleting native projections leaves the reconstructed current outcome unchanged. The source-head check selects current accepted context independently of the snapshot, never by newest filename or cache timestamp. Projection payload object/event references are prior-only and must match replay at that head. Data 20 separately defines its raw export wrapper. Paragraph 3 is a separator.

Remaining proof: all independently qualified channel/capture/freshness/boundary context; correct initial and successor grant/exposure/knowledge transactions; complete channel accounting and conservative unknown handling; exact role-specific admission and contamination applicability; delivery/capability and full resolver closure; snapshot replay and stale-state rejection; retained raw bytes; immutable revision/concurrency behavior and durable atomic rejection. No enforcement, knowledge status, scientific PASS or qualification is established by profile possession or these model mappings.
